# UK privacy policy checklist (UK GDPR Art. 13, PECR, ICO guidance)

Check the policy text for each item. Report only what is missing or wrong, in plain terms.

1. **Controller identity and contact**: legal entity name, postal address, an email (a role address such as
   privacy@ is preferable to a personal mailbox), company number if a company.
2. **Purposes and lawful basis for each**: contract (orders), legal obligation (tax records), legitimate
   interests (fraud prevention, basic analytics if justified), consent (marketing email, ad tracking).
   Stock Shopify, Wix and Squarespace templates usually omit lawful bases.
3. **Recipients by name or category**: compare with `privacy.json` → `accept.thirdParty` hosts and the app
   scripts. Typical gaps: Meta (Facebook pixel), Google (Analytics, Ads, Merchant Center), Klaviyo or Mailchimp,
   review apps (Reviews.io, Yotpo, Trustpilot), subscription apps (Appstle, Recharge), payment providers.
4. **International transfers**: the mechanism (UK IDTA, Addendum to EU SCCs, UK–US Data Bridge).
5. **Retention periods**: specific periods or criteria per category (e.g. order records 6 years for HMRC).
   "Depends on factors" is not enough.
6. **Data subject rights (UK list)**: access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection,
   withdrawal of consent, and how to exercise them.
7. **Right to complain to the ICO**, named, with a link to ico.org.uk. EEA-only regulator references are a gap.
8. **Automated decision-making and profiling**: say if none.
9. **Cookies**: a cookie list or policy (names, purpose, duration, first or third party) linked from the banner.
10. **Jurisdiction mismatch**: US-only wording ("sell/share", "authorized agent", CCPA) suggests an unadapted
    template. Low on its own, but it signals the items above need checking.
11. **Contradictions**, e.g. "call us" with no phone number, or a last-updated date before apps that are now
    running were installed.
